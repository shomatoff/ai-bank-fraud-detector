# Banking integration roadmap — no real-bank integration in this release

This repository is a **synthetic, local lab**. It must not be pointed at bank records, live payment APIs or customer systems; it cannot authorize, reject, block, reverse or execute any transaction. The local analyst status is a simulator annotation, not a bank case state. [Capabilities and limitations](FRAUD_DETECTION_CAPABILITIES.md), [data card](DATA_CARD.md), [model card](MODEL_CARD.md), [evaluation](MODEL_EVALUATION.md), and [demo](DEMO_WALKTHROUGH.md) define this release.

Any future, separately authorized integration would require a bank-owned scope and legal basis, a data-protection impact assessment, minimization/pseudonymization, consent or other lawful basis, retention/deletion rules, encryption in transit and at rest, secrets management, threat modeling, dependency/SBOM review, authenticated role-based access with least privilege, separation of duties, and tamper-evident centralized audit logs. This local CSRF/Host check and SQLite file are not substitutes.

A safe research sequence would be: (1) agree feature definitions and event-time semantics with data governance; (2) reproduce historical features *as known at decision time*, accounting for late-arriving data and delayed fraud labels; (3) use a read-only offline backtest with temporal and institution-level holdouts; (4) run a **read-only shadow evaluation** that observes but never affects live decisions; (5) review precision, recall, calibration, alert capacity, customer harm, fraud loss and costs with analysts; (6) test slices and legally relevant fairness/bias concerns; (7) monitor PSI and richer drift/outcome indicators with approved trigger thresholds and rollback; (8) only then consider a separately designed human-approved operational workflow under bank controls. No stage grants this repository authority to access or act on live transactions.

Prerequisites also include model/version provenance, signed artifact distribution (SHA256 alone is insufficient), security testing, CI supply-chain controls, backup/recovery, incident response, availability targets, legal/compliance review, human appeal and override, change approval, and periodic independent validation. Real bank integration and automatic card/payment blocking are **NOT IMPLEMENTED** here. Neither high synthetic PR-AUC nor a Dockerfile changes that boundary.
