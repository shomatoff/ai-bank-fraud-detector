I built a local AI Bank Fraud Detector laboratory for explainable transaction-risk analysis on synthetic data.

The project uses Python, pandas/NumPy, scikit-learn, FastAPI, SQLite, HTML/CSS/JavaScript, pytest and Ruff. Its current ten-seed synthetic audit reports mean precision **0.8313**, recall **0.6689** and PR-AUC **0.7455** at the documented alert policy, with a held-out card-testing recall of **0.0127**. These figures are simulator results, not bank performance or evidence of fraud.

The UI is available in English, Uzbek and Russian. It runs locally, stores synthetic analyst reports locally, and has no live banking connection or automatic blocking. The repository is a portfolio learning project about temporal evaluation, leakage control, explainability and honest limitations.
