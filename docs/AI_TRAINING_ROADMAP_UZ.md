# AI modelini nimaga o‘qitish kerak: manbali bosqichma-bosqich reja

Sana: 2026-10-18. Holat: **1-bosqich (mavjud ma’lumot auditi) bajarildi; tashqi datasetlarda model o‘qitilmadi.** Bu hujjat tavsiya va tajriba rejasidir; ilovaning joriy modeli, sxemasi yoki natijalarini almashtirmaydi.

## Hozirgi aniq holat

`fraud_detector/event_stream.py` seed 42 bilan 12 000 sintetik tranzaksiya yaratdi: 756 `is_fraud=1` (6,3%), 0 bo‘sh katak. Modelga 10 belgi kiradi: `amount`, `hour`, `merchant_category`, `channel`, `country_risk`, `is_new_device`, `velocity_1h`, `distance_km`, `account_age_days`, `prior_declines_24h`; `scenario`, `customer_id`, `event_time`, `is_fraud` model belgisi emas. Fit/calibration/validation vaqt ketma-ketligida 7 200/2 400/2 400 qatorga ajraladi. Fit va validation to‘plamlarida 563 ta sintetik customer ID bir xil: bu aynan takroriy mijozning kelajakdagi tranzaksiyasini baholashga mos, ammo **butunlay yangi mijozga umumlashishni** o‘lchamaydi. Alohida customer-disjoint test kerak.

`data/v6_evaluation.json` va [MODEL_EVALUATION](MODEL_EVALUATION.md)ga ko‘ra, 10 sintetik test seedida 5% aniq alert hajmida precision o‘rtacha **0,8313**, recall **0,6689**, PR-AUC **0,7455**. Oldin ko‘rilmagan `card_testing` ssenariyida recall **5/393 = 0,0127**. Bu simulyator natijasi; real bankdagi aniqlik emas. Joriy sinovlarda fraud ulushi 6,3% atrofida, [ULB sahifasidagi](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mlg-ulb/creditcardfraud/data) ulush esa 492/284 807 = 0,172%; vazifalar va kelib chiqish turlicha, shuning uchun bu ikki raqamni model sifati sifatida bevosita solishtirib bo‘lmaydi.

## Eng muhim 10 tavsiya

1. **O‘qitish maqsadini aniq belgilang:** “analitik ko‘radigan eng yuqori riskli sintetik tranzaksiyalarni saralash”; model avtomatik bloklash yoki real zarar da’vosini qilmasin.
2. **Asosiy 10 belgini faqat voqea paytida mavjud bo‘lsa oling.** `scenario`, label, kelajakdagi velocity, keyingi rad javobi, keyingi balans va tekshiruv natijasi kirmasin. [scikit-learn leakage qo‘llanmasi](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html).
3. **Qonuniy “qiyin salbiy” misollarni ko‘paytiring:** sayohat, katta xarid, yangi qurilma, ko‘p rad etish. Har biridan kelgan false alertni alohida ko‘rsating; katta negative soni bilan metrikani bezamang.
4. **Card testingni ikki xil baholang:** tanish naqshlar treningda bo‘lsin, biroq bir butun yangi card-testing naqshini holdout qoldiring. Yangi naqshda recall hozir 0,0127; yangi yondashuv shu raqamga va false alertga birga ta’siri bilan baholansin.
5. **Vaqt bo‘yicha fit → calibration → validation → kechroq test tartibini saqlang;** qo‘shimcha ravishda customer-disjoint test o‘tkazing. Treningdan oldin transformerni barcha satrlarga fit qilmang. [ULB handbook validation](https://fraud-detection-handbook.github.io/fraud-detection-handbook/Chapter_5_ModelValidationAndSelection/Introduction.html).
6. **ULB, PaySim va IEEE-CIS uchun alohida model/benchmark yuriting.** Ularning label ma’nosi, domeni, features va litsenziyasi boshqa; bitta “ko‘proq data” deb qo‘shish asossiz transfer natija beradi.
7. **Oddiy baseline’ni saqlang:** always-legitimate, muzlatilgan qoida, logistika va bitta kichik tree/boosting. Murakkab model faqat kechroq, ayni holdoutda aniq foyda bersa qo‘shilsin.
8. **Accuracy o‘rniga PR-AUC, prevalence, precision/recall @ analitik sig‘imi, FP/FN, FPR va kalibrlashni birga bering.** Top-k retrospektiv ranking bilan validatsiyada tanlangan real threshold natijasini alohida belgilang. [ULB dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mlg-ulb/creditcardfraud/data), [ULB top-k metodologiyasi](https://fraud-detection-handbook.github.io/fraud-detection-handbook/Chapter_4_PerformanceMetrics/TopKBased.html).
9. **Threshold va calibrationni faqat validationda tanlang;** testga qarab qayta sozlashdan oldin yangi holdout ajrating. Qayta o‘qitish uchun dataset versiyasi, checksum, vaqt oralig‘i, split va seed yozilsin.
10. **Har bir sinov uchun “go/no-go” qaydi tuting:** ayniqsa yangi ssenariy, yangi customer, qonuniy qiyin holatlar va vaqt siljishida xatolarni ko‘ring. Natija yomonlashsa ishlab turgan modelni almashtirmang. [NIST AI RMF](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/); model boshqaruviga misol sifatida [Federal Reserve 2026 SR 26-2](https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/sr2602.htm) (u asosan yirik banklarga mo‘ljallangan, bu portfolio uchun majburiy standart emas).
